About Us

Who we are

CT Foundation is a non-profit organisation committed to holistic human development. We partner with communities, organisations and well-wishers to design and implement sustainable solutions in education, healthcare, livelihood and empowerment.

Our Mission

To create lasting impact through innovation, collaboration and compassion, enabling individuals and communities to build better lives and brighter futures.
